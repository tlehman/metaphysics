Twenty years in [Plato](plato.md)'s Academy, then the Lyceum. What the
[*Metaphysics*](../aristotle/metaphysics-treatise.md) contributes is less a set
of doctrines than a working vocabulary, most of it still in use:

- [substance](../aristotle/substance.md) and the
  [categories](../aristotle/categories.md);
- [essence](../aristotle/essence.md) and definition;
- [matter and form](../aristotle/hylomorphism.md);
- [potentiality and actuality](../aristotle/potentiality-and-actuality.md);
- the [four causes](../aristotle/four-causes.md);
- the [principle of non-contradiction](../aristotle/principle-of-non-contradiction.md).

The two features that make him hard to pin down are also what make him
generative: he begins inquiries by [cataloguing the
perplexities](../aristotle/aristotelian-aporiai.md) and arguing both sides, and
he holds that key terms are [said in many
ways](../aristotle/pros-hen-equivocity.md) rather than univocal. Both traits
mean the treatise poses more than it settles — the
[universals problem](../aristotle/universals-and-substance.md) is unresolved on
the page.

Reception splits early and never rejoins: the Neoplatonists absorb him into a
hierarchy topped by [the One](../ultimate-reality/the-one.md);
[Avicenna](avicenna.md) and [Aquinas](aquinas.md) rebuild him around
[existence](../ultimate-reality/essence-existence-distinction.md);
the early moderns define themselves against him; and
[Heidegger](heidegger.md) reads the whole tradition, Aristotle included, as
having [forgotten the question](../being/ontological-difference.md) it thought
it was answering.
