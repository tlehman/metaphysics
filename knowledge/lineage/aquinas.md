Aquinas is often read as Aristotle baptised, which undersells the change. The
addition is *esse*: in every creature there is a real composition of
[essence and existence](../ultimate-reality/essence-existence-distinction.md),
and existence is not one more form but the actuality by which any form is at all.

That single move rearranges everything:

- **God is *ipsum esse subsistens*** — subsistent being itself, whose essence is
  His existence. Hence [pure actuality](../ultimate-reality/pure-actuality.md)
  and [divine simplicity](../ultimate-reality/divine-simplicity.md), from which
  the attributes follow.
- **Creatures *participate*** in being rather than possessing it — Neoplatonic
  vocabulary ([emanation](../ultimate-reality/neoplatonic-emanation.md)) doing
  work inside an Aristotelian ontology, but with creation free rather than
  necessary.
- **The *prima via*** takes [Aristotle's argument from
  motion](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md) and the later ways turn it into an
  argument about the dependence of *being* — see
  [cosmological argument](../ultimate-reality/cosmological-argument.md).
- **Hylomorphism applied to the soul**: the rational soul is the substantial
  form of the body, one form per human being — a hard position to hold together
  with its subsistence, and the point on which the Aristotelian and Christian
  commitments strain most.
