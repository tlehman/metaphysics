The *Shifāʾ* ("Healing") reorganises Aristotle rather than commenting on him,
and the reorganisation is what matters. Aristotle's subject was
[being qua being](../aristotle/being-qua-being.md) with
[substance](../aristotle/substance.md) at the centre; Avicenna keeps that and
adds the question Aristotle never asked — why does any of it exist?

- **[Essence and existence](../ultimate-reality/essence-existence-distinction.md)**
  come apart. A nature considered in itself is neither existent nor
  non-existent, so existence is something received.
- **Possible vs. necessary in itself.** What does not exist by its own essence
  needs a cause; the series terminates in the Necessary Existent, whose essence
  is its existence. This is a
  [cosmological argument](../ultimate-reality/cosmological-argument.md) from the
  structure of being, not from motion — a different argument from
  [Λ's](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md), reaching a different kind of conclusion.
- **Emanationist cosmology.** The Necessary Existent's self-knowledge produces
  the first intellect, and so down through the celestial spheres — Neoplatonic
  machinery ([emanation](../ultimate-reality/neoplatonic-emanation.md)) on an
  Aristotelian frame.

Al-Ghazālī attacked the necessity of emanation as incompatible with creation;
Averroes attacked the essence/existence distinction as a logician's confusion.
Both attacks travelled to the Latin West with the doctrine, and
[Aquinas](aquinas.md) sides with Avicenna on the distinction while siding with
al-Ghazālī on free creation.
