Everything in the Aristotelian arc is a reply to a position Plato holds, and the
positions worth isolating here are three.

- **[The Forms](../ultimate-reality/theory-of-forms.md)** — separate, eternal,
  non-sensible paradigms, posited because knowledge needs a stable object.
- **The Good beyond being** (*Republic* 509b) — a first principle above the
  Forms, which [Plotinus](plotinus.md) takes literally and builds
  [the One](../ultimate-reality/the-one.md) from.
- **Mathematics as the training for dialectic** (*Republic* VII) — the objects of
  geometry are intermediate between Forms and sensibles, which is where
  [mathematical platonism](../mathematics/mathematical-platonism.md) starts.

Aristotle spent twenty years in the Academy, and the *Metaphysics*'s
anti-Platonic arguments (Α.9, Μ–Ν) are those of someone arguing with his own
teachers, not with a caricature: Plato's *Parmenides* had already raised the
participation and regress problems Aristotle presses.
