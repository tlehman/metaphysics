# lineage

The philosophers, each entered by what they changed rather than by biography — Plato to Heidegger, tracked across all three arcs.

## Concepts

* [Thomas Aquinas](aquinas.md) - 1225–1274. Who made *esse*, the act of being, the actuality of all acts — synthesising Aristotle's hylomorphism, Avicenna's essence/existence distinction and Neoplatonic participation into a metaphysics of creation.
* [Aristotle](aristotle.md) - 384–322 BCE. The source of this bundle — of the vocabulary of substance, essence, form, matter, potentiality and actuality that the next two thousand years of metaphysics argues in.
* [Avicenna (Ibn Sīnā)](avicenna.md) - c. 970–1037. Whose distinction between essence and existence, and division of beings into the possible and the necessary in itself, made Aristotle's metaphysics into an account of why anything exists at all.
* [Gottlob Frege](frege.md) - 1848–1925. Inventor of modern logic, who tried to show arithmetic is analytic by deriving it from logic alone, and whose failure and its repairs organise the philosophy of mathematics.
* [Martin Heidegger](heidegger.md) - 1889–1976. Who charged the tradition with forgetting the question of being by always answering it with a being — and read Aristotle's identification of first philosophy with theology as the founding instance.
* [Immanuel Kant](kant.md) - 1724–1804. Whose critique made metaphysics a question about the conditions of possible experience rather than about being — ending the rationalist project of knowing the unconditioned, and setting the problem logicism was designed to solve.
* [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz](leibniz.md) - 1646–1716. Who made the principle of sufficient reason a foundation coordinate with non-contradiction, asked why there is something rather than nothing, and spent his system's ingenuity avoiding Spinoza's answer.
* [Plato](plato.md) - c. 428–348 BCE. Founder of the Academy, whose separation of the objects of knowledge from the sensible world set the problem the *Metaphysics* is written to solve.
* [Plotinus](plotinus.md) - 204/5–270 CE. Founder of Neoplatonism, who placed a principle beyond being and beyond thought above Aristotle's self-thinking god, and derived everything else from it by emanation.
* [W. V. O. Quine](quine.md) - 1908–2000. Who gave ontology a criterion — to be is to be the value of a bound variable — and, with holism and naturalism, made mathematics' indispensability to science an argument for its objects.
* [Baruch Spinoza](spinoza.md) - 1632–1677. Who took the Aristotelian definition of substance seriously enough to conclude there is exactly one, identical with God and with Nature, and that everything follows from it with the necessity of geometry.
