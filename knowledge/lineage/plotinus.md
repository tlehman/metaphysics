The *Enneads* — arranged by Porphyry into six sets of nine — read Plato as a
systematic metaphysician and absorb Aristotle as a subordinate authority.

The decisive move is the demotion of self-thinking thought. Aristotle's
[unmoved mover](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md) is *noêsis noêseôs*, but thinking
distinguishes thinker from thought, so it is already two;
[the One](../ultimate-reality/the-one.md) must be prior to that. Aristotle's god
thereby becomes *Nous*, the second hypostasis, and the
[Forms](../ultimate-reality/theory-of-forms.md) are relocated inside it as its
contents.

From there: [emanation](../ultimate-reality/neoplatonic-emanation.md) without
diminution, a descending series each level of which is constituted by
contemplating its source, and matter as the point where the outflow fades.

The influence runs through Proclus and the *Liber de Causis* into Islamic and
Christian metaphysics — which is why [Aquinas](aquinas.md) argues against a
"Platonism" he mostly meets in Neoplatonic form, and why
[divine simplicity](../ultimate-reality/divine-simplicity.md) has a
Plotinian shape even in authors who reject the hypostases.
