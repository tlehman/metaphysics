The *Ethics* (1677) is written *more geometrico* and reaches
[monism](../being/substance-monism.md) inside the first fifteen propositions,
using nothing but the scholastic definition of substance turned against
scholastic pluralism.

The chain: a substance is *in itself and conceived through itself*; two
substances cannot share an attribute (they would be indiscernible); a substance
cannot be produced by another (it would then be conceived through it); so
substance is self-caused and infinite; so there is exactly one, *Deus sive
Natura*, with infinite attributes of which we know two, thought and extension.
Everything else is a *mode* — a way the one substance is.

Set against [Aristotle](aristotle.md), the vocabulary is identical and the
conclusion inverted: independence, which Aristotle used to pick out *many*
[substances](../aristotle/substance.md), turns out on strict reading to be
satisfiable only once.

Two consequences the tradition found intolerable, and which are the price of
taking the [PSR](../ultimate-reality/principle-of-sufficient-reason.md) without
exceptions:

- **Necessitarianism.** Nothing could have been otherwise; God does not choose.
- **No final causes.** Teleology is an anthropomorphic projection (the Appendix
  to Part I), which discards
  [Aristotle's final cause](../aristotle/four-causes.md) outright.

Schaffer's [priority monism](../being/substance-monism.md) is the contemporary
descendant that keeps the structure and drops the necessitarianism.
