Every explanation invites another question, and the third arc is about what
happens at the end. Either the chain terminates, or it regresses, or it loops,
or the demand is illegitimate. Each option has a serious defender.

| Shape | Claim | Instance |
|---|---|---|
| **Necessary being** | something exists that could not have failed to | [cosmological argument](cosmological-argument.md), [pure actuality](pure-actuality.md) |
| **Brute fact** | something is simply the case with no explanation | rejection of the [PSR](principle-of-sufficient-reason.md) |
| **Self-explanation** | the terminus explains itself | [Spinoza](../lineage/spinoza.md)'s *causa sui*, [divine simplicity](divine-simplicity.md) |
| **Dissolution** | the demand is malformed | [Kant](../lineage/kant.md); [Heidegger's diagnosis](../being/ontological-difference.md) |

The engine driving the whole arc is the
[principle of sufficient reason](principle-of-sufficient-reason.md): accept it
and brute facts are ruled out, so one of the other three must hold; reject it
and the argument stops before it starts.

## Aristotle's version

The [unmoved mover](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md) is the first fully worked
terminus in the tradition, and it is not what later theism made of it: it is
required by the argument that eternal motion cannot be caused by anything whose
substance is a mere *capacity*
([priority of actuality](../aristotle/priority-of-actuality.md)), it moves as an
object of desire rather than by pushing, and it does not create. What made it
theologically usable was the [essence/existence
distinction](essence-existence-distinction.md), which let
[Avicenna](../lineage/avicenna.md) and [Aquinas](../lineage/aquinas.md) convert a
cause of *motion* into a cause of *being*.

The modern candidates are on the other arcs: reality as
[structure](ontic-structural-realism.md), or reality as
[mathematics](../mathematics/mathematical-universe-hypothesis.md), each proposing
a terminus that needs no theology.
