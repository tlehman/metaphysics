# Log

## 2026-09-02

- **Creation** — third arc, eleven concepts, hubbed on
  [ultimate explanation](ultimate-explanation.md) and its four possible shapes
  (necessary being, brute fact, self-explanation, dissolution).
- **Note** — the arc's spine is the transformation of
  [Aristotle's mover](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md), which explains eternal
  *motion*, into a cause of *being*, via
  [the essence/existence distinction](essence-existence-distinction.md). That
  transformation is the single most consequential edit the tradition made to the
  seed text, so it gets its own node rather than a paragraph inside
  [Avicenna](../lineage/avicenna.md).
- **Note** — [ontic structural realism](ontic-structural-realism.md) closes the
  arc with a terminus that has no theology in it, and is written as the exact
  negation of [substance](../aristotle/substance.md).
