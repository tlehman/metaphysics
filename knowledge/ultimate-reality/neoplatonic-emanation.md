The problem: if [the One](the-one.md) is utterly simple, how does anything else
arise? Not by choice (that introduces deliberation and so duality), not by
motion (the One does not change), and not by diminution.

Plotinus's answer is the metaphor that names the doctrine — light from the sun,
heat from fire, fragrance from a flower. Whatever is perfect necessarily
produces, and produces without being diminished. Production is automatic,
eternal, and costless.

## The hypostases

| Level | Character |
|---|---|
| **The One** | beyond being and thought |
| **Nous** (Intellect) | being and thinking coincide; contains the [Forms](theory-of-forms.md); this is where [Aristotle's god](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md) lands in the hierarchy |
| **Soul** (*Psychê*) | discursive, temporal; produces and orders the sensible world |
| **Nature / matter** | the last trace, where the outflow fades |

Each level is generated by an indeterminate "procession" (*proodos*) from the
one above and constituted by "reversion" (*epistrophê*) — turning back to
contemplate its source. Contemplation is thus the fundamental productive act,
which inverts the ordinary relation between thinking and making.

## Why it matters downstream

Emanation is the chief rival to *creatio ex nihilo*: necessary and eternal
rather than free and temporal, and this is precisely the point on which
[Avicenna](../lineage/avicenna.md) — who works with an emanationist
cosmology — was condemned by al-Ghazālī and corrected by
[Aquinas](../lineage/aquinas.md), who kept the metaphysics of
[participation](divine-simplicity.md) while insisting creation is free.

It is also the ancient template for a structure that recurs: a single ungrounded
source with everything else [grounded](../being/grounding.md) in it, which is
[priority monism](../being/substance-monism.md) with a theological ceiling.
