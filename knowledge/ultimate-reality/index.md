# ultimate-reality

Where explanation stops. The unmoved mover, the One, necessary being, and the modern candidates for a terminus that needs no theology.

## Concepts

* [The Cosmological Argument](cosmological-argument.md) - The family of arguments from the existence or contingency of the world to a first cause or necessary being — Aristotelian in origin, transformed by Avicenna and Aquinas from an argument about motion into one about existence.
* [Divine Simplicity](divine-simplicity.md) - That God has no parts of any kind — no matter, no distinct attributes, no composition of essence and existence — so that God is identical with His nature and His attributes are identical with each other.
* [The Essence/Existence Distinction](essence-existence-distinction.md) - Avicenna's separation of *what* a thing is from *that* it is, so that existence is something a nature receives — the medieval addition to Aristotle that converts a cause of motion into a cause of being.
* [Emanation](neoplatonic-emanation.md) - The Neoplatonic account of how multiplicity derives from the One without creation, deliberation, or loss — an overflow of superabundance, structured as a descending series each level of which contemplates the one above it.
* [Ontic Structural Realism](ontic-structural-realism.md) - That structure is all there is at the fundamental level — relations without relata, or relata with no nature beyond their relational profile — proposed to explain why physics survives theory change and why quantum particles resist individuation.
* [The Principle of Sufficient Reason](principle-of-sufficient-reason.md) - That everything that is has a reason why it is and is as it is — Leibniz's second great principle, which drives the rationalist system to necessitarianism and whose denial concedes brute facts.
* [Pure Actuality](pure-actuality.md) - *Actus purus* — a being with no unrealised potentiality whatever, which follows from Aristotle's argument that an eternal cause cannot have a capacity as its substance, and which the scholastics use to derive immutability, eternity and simplicity at once.
* [The One](the-one.md) - Plotinus's first principle, beyond being and beyond thought — posited because anything that thinks is already two, so the source of all multiplicity must be prior to the distinction between knower and known.
* [Plato's Theory of Forms](theory-of-forms.md) - That the objects of knowledge are eternal, unchanging, non-sensible paradigms existing separately from their instances — the position Aristotle's *Metaphysics* is written against, and the ancestor of every platonism since.
* [Ultimate Explanation](ultimate-explanation.md) - The hub of the third arc — the demand for a terminus of explanation that does not itself require explaining, and the four standard shapes such a terminus can take.
* [Why Is There Something Rather Than Nothing?](why-anything-at-all.md) - Leibniz's question, and the family of replies — that nothing is impossible, that nothing is improbable, that the question presupposes an illegitimate demand, and that something exists necessarily.
