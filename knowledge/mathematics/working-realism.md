Not a thesis about what exists or what is true, but about how to proceed. The
working realist endorses the classical methods:

- **Classical logic**, so excluded middle holds unrestrictedly over infinite
  domains — $\varphi \vee \neg\varphi$ regardless of whether either disjunct is
  provable.
- **Non-constructive existence proofs**: establishing $\exists x\,\Phi(x)$
  without exhibiting any witness.
- **Impredicative definitions**: defining an object by quantifying over a
  totality that includes it, as the least upper bound is defined by quantifying
  over all upper bounds.

Each of these is legitimate *if* the objects are there in advance, waiting to be
described, and suspect if mathematics is a construction. Hence the name.

## Why the distinction matters

Working realism is what almost every practising mathematician holds, and it is
almost entirely uninformative about philosophy. It is strictly weaker than
[truth-value realism](truth-value-realism.md), which is in turn weaker than
[platonism](mathematical-platonism.md):

$$\text{platonism} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{truth-value realism} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{working realism}$$

and none of the converses hold. A [fictionalist](fictionalism.md) is a working
realist — the fiction is a classical one, so reasoning inside it uses classical
logic. A [formalist](formalism.md) is a working realist about whichever formal
system is on the table. Even a [nominalist](mathematical-nominalism.md) who
denies there is anything for the quantifiers to range over will still prove
theorems the classical way.

The one position that genuinely rejects working realism is
[intuitionism](intuitionism.md), and it rejects it precisely *because* it takes
the methodological question to be settled by the metaphysical one — which is why
Brouwer's programme demands revising mathematics rather than merely
reinterpreting it.

## The moral

Arguments that run from mathematical practice to platonism have to cross this
gap, and mostly cannot. That mathematicians reason as if the objects were there
is evidence about the convenience of a method, not about a realm. The
[indispensability argument](indispensability-argument.md) is the serious attempt
to bridge it, and it goes through *science* rather than through practice for
exactly this reason.
