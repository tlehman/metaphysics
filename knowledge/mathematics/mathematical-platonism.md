Three conjuncts, each doing work:

1. **Existence.** There are mathematical objects.
2. **Abstractness.** They are [abstract](abstract-objects.md) — outside space
   and time, causally inert.
3. **Independence.** They do not depend on us, our language, or our practices.

Drop (3) and you get some form of constructivism
([intuitionism](intuitionism.md)); drop (2) and you get a physicalist or
psychologistic view; drop (1) and you get
[nominalism](mathematical-nominalism.md) or
[fictionalism](fictionalism.md).

## The case for

- **Face value.** "There are infinitely many primes" is a true sentence of the
  standard form, and semantics should not be rewritten to avoid its obvious
  reading.
- **[Indispensability](indispensability-argument.md).** Our best science
  quantifies over mathematical objects and cannot be paraphrased free of them;
  by [Quine's criterion](../being/ontological-commitment.md), we are committed.
- **Objectivity.** Mathematical practice treats open questions as having
  determinate answers we have not yet found — which is easiest to explain if
  there is something the answers are about.

## The case against

The [epistemological objection](benacerrafs-dilemma.md) is the serious one: if
the objects are causally isolated, no causal story about how our beliefs track
them is available, and it is unclear what non-causal story could do the job.
Field sharpens it — the platonist owes an explanation of the *reliability* of
mathematicians' beliefs, and cannot even sketch one.

Second: the [identification problem](structuralism.md). If 2 is a set, which
set? Zermelo's {{∅}} and von Neumann's {∅,{∅}} both work perfectly and
disagree, so no fact seems to select one — which is Benacerraf's other paper and
the birth of [structuralism](structuralism.md).

## Aristotle's objection, which is still live

Aristotle's whole treatment ([Μ–Ν](../aristotle/mathematical-abstractionism.md))
is aimed at exactly this position: mathematical objects are not
[separate](../glossary/choriston.md) substances, they are perceptible magnitudes
considered as separated. Compare
[the theory of Forms](../ultimate-reality/theory-of-forms.md), which is where the
name comes from — though modern platonism keeps the abstractness and drops the
Forms' causal and evaluative roles entirely.
