# Log

## 2026-09-03

- **Addition** — six nodes worked out of the SEP entry on
  [platonism in the philosophy of mathematics](../references/sep-platonism-mathematics.md):
  [the Fregean argument](fregean-argument.md),
  [truth-value realism](truth-value-realism.md),
  [working realism](working-realism.md),
  [plenitudinous platonism](plenitudinous-platonism.md),
  [potentialism](mathematical-potentialism.md), and
  [the reliability challenge](reliability-challenge.md).
- **Note** — the arc previously had one argument for platonism
  ([indispensability](indispensability-argument.md)); the Fregean argument is
  the other, and unlike it needs no premises about science.
- **Note** — platonism is no longer treated as a single position. The realism
  ladder (working realism → truth-value realism → object realism → platonism)
  and the counterfactual/robust reading of Independence are now recorded in
  [mathematical platonism](mathematical-platonism.md); arguments that slide
  between rungs are the arc's most common failure mode.
- **Note** — [potentialism](mathematical-potentialism.md) is a third bridge
  between the Aristotelian core and the mathematical arc, alongside
  [abstractionism](../aristotle/mathematical-abstractionism.md): it is
  [dunamis](../glossary/dunamis.md) applied to the infinite, and it strains
  [the priority of actuality](../aristotle/priority-of-actuality.md).
- **Note** — first use of LaTeX in the bundle. Formal statements of the
  platonist conjuncts, the Fregean schema, the plenitude principle and the
  potentialist modal translation are written as math rather than prose, and
  render in the viz through KaTeX.
