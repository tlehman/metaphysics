Three questions organise the field, and every position is a package deal across
all three.

| Question | Where it leads |
|---|---|
| **Ontology.** Do numbers, sets and structures exist, and if so how? | [platonism](mathematical-platonism.md), [nominalism](mathematical-nominalism.md), [structuralism](structuralism.md), [fictionalism](fictionalism.md) |
| **Epistemology.** How could beings like us know about them? | [Benacerraf's dilemma](benacerrafs-dilemma.md), [intuitionism](intuitionism.md) |
| **Application.** Why does it work on the physical world? | [indispensability](indispensability-argument.md), [unreasonable effectiveness](unreasonable-effectiveness.md) |

Answering any two well tends to wreck the third. That is the field's central
structural fact, and [Benacerraf's dilemma](benacerrafs-dilemma.md) is its
sharpest statement.

## Why it belongs in a metaphysics bundle

Not by analogy. The question is one of Aristotle's own
[aporiai](../aristotle/aristotelian-aporiai.md) — "are mathematical objects
substances, and if so are they separate from sensible things?" — and Books Μ–Ν
are devoted to answering it in the negative
([mathematical abstractionism](../aristotle/mathematical-abstractionism.md)).
The modern versions of the same fight are the same fight: numbers are the
cleanest case of [abstract objects](abstract-objects.md), and the realism
dispute about them is the realism dispute about
[universals](../being/universals-and-particulars.md) with the empirical noise
removed.

The foundational programmes — [logicism](logicism.md),
[formalism](formalism.md), [intuitionism](intuitionism.md) — were attempts to
settle all three questions at once by settling what mathematics *is*, and their
partial failure is why the ontological questions came back. Two results did the
damage: [Russell's paradox](russells-paradox.md), which showed that set
existence cannot be free, and [Gödel's theorems](godel-incompleteness.md), which
showed that consistency cannot be certified from inside. What replaced the free
principle — [type theory](type-theory.md) or
[limitation of size](limitation-of-size.md) — is itself a metaphysical choice
about what collections there are.

At the far end, [structural realism](../ultimate-reality/ontic-structural-realism.md)
and the [mathematical universe hypothesis](mathematical-universe-hypothesis.md)
propose that the application question has been backwards all along.
