The claim that every well-formed mathematical statement is determinately true or
determinately false, independently of what anyone can prove, know, or decide:

$$\forall \varphi\,\bigl(\mathrm{True}(\varphi) \vee \mathrm{True}(\neg\varphi)\bigr) \quad\text{and}\quad \mathrm{True}(\varphi) \text{ is settled independently of us}$$

This is a *metaphysical* view without being an *ontological* one. It says how
mathematical truth behaves; it says nothing about what makes it so. That gap is
the whole point of the category.

## Why it comes apart from platonism

The two are logically independent in both directions, which is easy to miss:

| | Truth-value realist | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Platonist** | the standard package — objects settle the truth-values | a platonist about a domain with indeterminate truth-values, e.g. one who takes [CH](set-theory-and-foundations.md) to lack a truth-value while sets exist |
| **Not** | a nominalist who grounds bivalence in modal or structural facts rather than objects — [Hellman's](structuralism.md) modal structuralism | [intuitionism](intuitionism.md), which denies both |

The right-hand column is the interesting one. Modal structuralism keeps
objective truth-values while ranging over *possible* systems rather than actual
objects, so it is a truth-value realist nominalism. And a set-theoretic
pluralist can be an unembarrassed platonist about sets while holding that the
continuum hypothesis is simply not the kind of thing that has an answer.

## What it is for

It isolates what most working mathematicians actually care about. The objection
that [Benacerraf's dilemma](benacerrafs-dilemma.md) presses — how could we know
about causally inert objects? — is an objection to *ontology*. If the
mathematically load-bearing commitment is only to bivalence, the platonist may
be able to pay less. Whether the epistemological bill actually shrinks is
disputed: knowing a mind-independent truth-value looks about as hard as knowing
a mind-independent object, and the
[reliability challenge](reliability-challenge.md) is usually restated to apply
to both.

Compare [working realism](working-realism.md), which is weaker again — a
methodology rather than any thesis about truth.
