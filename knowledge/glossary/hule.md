Imported from the *Physics* into the study of substance, where it makes
individuals [hylomorphic compounds](../aristotle/hylomorphism.md) rather than
the unanalysable atoms of the [*Categories*](../aristotle/categories.md).

Matter is the subject of change: what bears contrary predicates at different
times and persists through generation and destruction. Stripped of all form it
becomes [prime matter](../aristotle/prime-matter.md), which is indefinite and
therefore no substance. Correlative: [morphê](morphe.md), [eidos](eidos.md).

*Hulê noêtê*, intelligible matter, is the version pure geometrical objects are
made of, and the tool Η.6 uses on
[the unity of definition](../aristotle/unity-of-definition.md).
