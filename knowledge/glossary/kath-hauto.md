"What belongs to a thing in respect of itself belongs to it in its essence"
(*Post. An.* 73a34–5). Contrasted with *kata sumbebêkos*, accidental.

*Pale man* is not a *kath' hauto legomenon* — pallor is accidental to a man —
which is why it is not identical with its essence and cannot be a primary
[essence](../aristotle/essence.md).

Fine's modern revival of essence as definitional rather than modal is a
recovery of exactly this notion:
[essence and modality](../being/essence-and-modality.md).
