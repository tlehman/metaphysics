"The end, that for which a thing is done" (*Physics* 194b33) — health, of
walking. See [the four causes](../aristotle/four-causes.md).

Θ.8's argument: "the activity is the end, and it is for the sake of this that
the capacity is acquired. For animals do not see in order that they may have
sight, rather they have sight in order that they may see" (1050a9–17). Hence
[the priority of actuality](../aristotle/priority-of-actuality.md).

[Spinoza](../lineage/spinoza.md) discards the notion entirely as an
anthropomorphic projection.
