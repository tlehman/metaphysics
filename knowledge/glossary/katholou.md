"Being predicated of many is what makes something a universal"
(*De Int.* 17a37). Only universals are definable (Ζ.11), and all scientific
knowledge is of universals — so if no universal is a
[substance](../aristotle/substance.md), science and reality come apart. That is
the [primacy dilemma](../aristotle/primacy-dilemma.md).

Contrast [kath' hekaston](kath-hekaston.md).
