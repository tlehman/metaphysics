The word is built from the verb "to be," so "beingness" or "entity" is closer
than "substance" — the Latin *substantia* ("standing under") imported the
subject-of-predication reading and quietly narrowed it.

Two uses to keep apart:

- **A substance** — one of the independent things, a *this*
  ([tode ti](tode-ti.md)); *prôtê ousia*, primary substance, in the
  [*Categories*](../aristotle/categories.md).
- **The substance of x** — what makes *x* the thing it is, which is what
  [Ζ asks after](../aristotle/substance.md) and answers with
  [form](../aristotle/substantial-form.md).
