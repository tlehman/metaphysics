title: archê — starting-point, principle
description: A beginning, origin, or first principle — what a demonstration starts from and what reality is built out of, which for a realist must be the same thing.

arc: aristotle

First philosophy studies "the primary causes ([aitia](aitia.md)) and the
starting-points (*archai*)" (Α.1, 981b28). Ζ.17 calls substance an *archê kai
aitia* of being (1041a9).

That *archai* must serve both roles at once — first principles of science *and*
basic constituents of reality — is what generates the
[primacy dilemma](../aristotle/primacy-dilemma.md).
