Two senses, distinguished at Θ.1 1046a12 and Θ.6 1048a25:

- **Power** — a starting-point of change in another thing, whose exercise is a
  [kinêsis](kinesis.md).
- **Potentiality** — a capacity to be in a different and more completed state,
  correlative with [energeia](energeia.md) rather than with movement. This one
  is indefinable (1048a37).

Matter is linked to potentiality, substance to actuality. See
[potentiality and actuality](../aristotle/potentiality-and-actuality.md).
