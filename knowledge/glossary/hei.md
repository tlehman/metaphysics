The operator that makes [being qua being](../aristotle/being-qua-being.md) a
coherent project rather than a study of a peculiar object. Studying *X qua Y* is
studying the consequences of being *Y*: a bronze triangle *qua* triangle has
angles summing to two right angles, and its bronze never enters.

The same operator defines what the mathematician does — things *qua* countable
and measurable — which is the core of
[Aristotle's account of mathematical objects](../aristotle/mathematical-abstractionism.md).
