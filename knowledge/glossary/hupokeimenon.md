"That of which the other things are said, but which is itself not further said of
any other thing" (1028b36).

The [*Categories*](../aristotle/categories.md) is concerned with subjects of
*predication*, the *Physics* with subjects of *change*. Ζ.3 runs the two
together and the [subject criterion](../aristotle/subject-criterion.md) fails as
a result.

*Substantia* — literally what stands under — is the Latin calque, which is why
[ousia](ousia.md) came into English carrying a meaning it did not originally
have.
