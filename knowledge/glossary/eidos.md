title: eidos — form / species

Two meanings, and Ζ turns on the difference. In the
[*Categories*](../aristotle/categories.md) and *Topics*, *eidos* is the species,
a secondary substance. In [Ζ](../aristotle/substantial-form.md) it is the form
predicated of matter — and a universal like *man*, formerly a species and a
secondary substance, is demoted to "a compound of a sort, of this account and
this matter taken universally" (1035b29–30).

So "the *eidos* is primary substance" means the *form*, not the species. See
[essence](../aristotle/essence.md), which corresponds to a species without being
one.
