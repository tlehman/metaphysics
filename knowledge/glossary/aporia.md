Book Β is a catalogue of them, argued on both sides — see
[the aporiai of Book Β](../aristotle/aristotelian-aporiai.md). Working through
an *aporia* is a method, not a preliminary: one cannot know the way out without
first knowing one is tied up.

The [primacy dilemma](../aristotle/primacy-dilemma.md) and
[the unity of definition](../aristotle/unity-of-definition.md) are the two the
treatise works hardest on.
