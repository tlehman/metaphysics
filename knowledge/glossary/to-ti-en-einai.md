Aristotle's own term for what a real definition states. He also uses the shorter
*to ti esti*, "the what it is."

The imperfect tense (*ên*, "was") is unexplained and much discussed; the usual
suggestion is that it marks what a thing is *all along*, independent of the
moment of asking.

See [essence](../aristotle/essence.md), [kath' hauto](kath-hauto.md), and the
contemporary dispute over
[essence and modality](../being/essence-and-modality.md).
