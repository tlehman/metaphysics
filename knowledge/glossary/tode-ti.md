Being a *tode ti* is being fully determinate. Being a
[kath' hekaston](kath-hekaston.md) is being particular and unrepeatable. The two
come apart, which leaves room for a **universal *tode ti*** — the manoeuvre
that lets [substantial form](../aristotle/substantial-form.md) survive
[Ζ.13](../aristotle/universals-and-substance.md).

Paired with [chôriston](choriston.md) in Ζ.3's requirement on
[substance](../aristotle/substance.md).
