*x* is *chôriston* from *y* if *x* can exist without *y*. The
[subject criterion](../aristotle/subject-criterion.md) founders because matter
can be separable (the wood outlasts the desk) but is then no definite
individual, or can be a definite individual and is then not separable from the
form making it one.

Separateness is also what Aristotle denies to mathematical objects: they are
studied *as* separated (*hôs kekhôrismenon*) without being so —
[abstractionism](../aristotle/mathematical-abstractionism.md) — and it is his
chief objection to [Plato's Forms](../ultimate-reality/theory-of-forms.md).
