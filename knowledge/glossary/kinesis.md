The housebuilder's craft is a power whose exercise is the *kinêsis* of building.
A *kinêsis* is incomplete while it goes on and ends when its goal is reached;
an [energeia](energeia.md) — seeing, understanding — is complete at every
moment.

The distinction matters for the [unmoved mover](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md),
whose life is *energeia* with no *kinêsis* in it at all. See
[dunamis](dunamis.md).
