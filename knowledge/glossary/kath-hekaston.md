Aristotle's label for particulars, against [katholou](katholou.md) for
universals. Particulars are indefinable (Ζ.15), which is the argument the SEP
authors take to settle that
[substantial forms are universals](../aristotle/universals-and-substance.md).

Not to be confused with [tode ti](tode-ti.md). Modern descendant:
[universals and particulars](../being/universals-and-particulars.md).
