"Everyone takes what is called wisdom (*sophia*) to be concerned with the
primary causes ([aitia](aitia.md)) and the starting-points
([archai](arche.md))" (Α.1, 981b28) — which is what
[first philosophy](../aristotle/first-philosophy.md) proposes to study.

*Nicomachean Ethics* X.6–8 makes contemplative *sophia* the highest human
activity, on the ground that it is the activity the
[unmoved mover](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md) eternally is.
