The four senses — material, formal, efficient, final — are four kinds of answer
to "why?", not four kinds of pushing. See
[the four causes](../aristotle/four-causes.md).

One thing can be a cause in several senses at once: "form, mover and *telos*
often coincide" (198a25). The soul is cause in three
(*De Anima* 415b10), which is what lets
[substantial form](../aristotle/substantial-form.md) be *the* cause of a
substance's being.
