*Energeia* is from *ergon*, [work or function](ergon.md) — being-at-work.
*Entelecheia* contains *telos* — being-at-completion. Aristotle uses them nearly
interchangeably in Θ.

Activity is to potentiality as "what is awake is to what is asleep, and what is
seeing to what has its eyes closed but has sight, and what has been shaped out
of the matter to the matter" (1048b1–3).

[Actuality is prior](../aristotle/priority-of-actuality.md) in account, in time
and in substance — and the first principle is a substance whose *very substance*
is *energeia*: the [unmoved mover](../aristotle/unmoved-mover.md), which the
scholastics render [actus purus](../ultimate-reality/pure-actuality.md).
