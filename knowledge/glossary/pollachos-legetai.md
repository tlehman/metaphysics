title: pollachôs legetai — said in many ways

Not a diagnosis of ambiguity but of *structured* multiplicity. The senses of a
*pollachôs legomenon* radiate from a focal case, which is why a single science
of them is possible: see
[pros hen equivocity](../aristotle/pros-hen-equivocity.md).

Book Δ, the philosophical lexicon, is largely a catalogue of such terms.
Applied to [being](on.md), to [aitia](aitia.md), to
[dunamis](dunamis.md), and to unity.
