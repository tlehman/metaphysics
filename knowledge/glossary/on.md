*To on* is being, *ta onta* the beings. The [*Categories*](../aristotle/categories.md)
opens by dividing *ta onta* into ten kinds;
[Γ](../aristotle/being-qua-being.md) studies them all *qua* onta.

The crucial claim about the word is that it is
[said in many ways](pollachos-legetai.md) without being merely ambiguous. See
[pros hen equivocity](../aristotle/pros-hen-equivocity.md).
