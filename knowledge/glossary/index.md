# glossary

Aristotle's Greek technical vocabulary, from the glossary of *Metaphysics* Book Δ and the SEP entry's own — the terms the rest of the tradition argues in, and the ones whose translation history caused the trouble.

## Concepts

* [aitia / aition — cause, explanation](aitia.md) - Better rendered "explanation" than "cause": what answers a why-question, and like *being*, said in many ways — four of them.
* [aporia — perplexity, impasse](aporia.md) - Literally "no way through"; a problem that ties thinking in knots, and for Aristotle the proper way to begin an inquiry.
* [archê — starting-point, principle](arche.md) - A beginning, origin, or first principle — what a demonstration starts from and what reality is built out of, which for a realist must be the same thing.
* [chôriston — separable](choriston.md) - Capable of existing independently of something else; one of the two conditions Ζ.3 requires of substance, which matter cannot satisfy at the same time as being a *this*.
* [dunamis — potentiality, power](dunamis.md) - Both a thing's power to produce change in another and — the sense Θ cares about — its capacity to be in a more completed state; indefinable, graspable only from cases.
* [eidos — form / species](eidos.md) - The word that means *species* (against genus) in the logical works and *form* (against matter) in the hylomorphic ones — an equivocation that causes real interpretive trouble in Ζ.
* [energeia / entelecheia — activity, actuality](energeia.md) - Aristotle's coinages for being-at-work and being-at-completion; what form becomes when the matter/form distinction is applied across time.
* [epistêmê — scientific knowledge](episteme.md) - Demonstrative knowledge of what cannot be otherwise, always of universals — which is what makes the particularity of substances a problem.
* [ergon — function, work](ergon.md) - What a thing characteristically does; the root of *energeia*, and what form is form *for*.
* [hêi — qua, in the respect that](hei.md) - The dative relative adverb behind "being qua being" — "X qua Y" is elliptical for "X in the respect that X is Y".
* [hulê — matter](hule.md) - Originally "wood" or timber; the stuff a compound is made of, correlative with form, and identified in Θ with potentiality.
* [hupokeimenon — subject, underlying thing](hupokeimenon.md) - "That which lies under" — what predicates are said of and what change happens to; translated *subiectum*, and the source of "subject" in both senses.
* [kath' hauto — per se, in respect of itself](kath-hauto.md) - What belongs to a thing intrinsically rather than accidentally — the mode of predication that marks off essential from accidental attributes.
* [kath' hekaston — particular](kath-hekaston.md) - What is unrepeatable and predicated of nothing else — contrasted with *katholou*, the universal.
* [katholou — universal](katholou.md) - What is "said of many"; the definable, and therefore the object of scientific knowledge — which is exactly why its exclusion from substance in Ζ.13 is a crisis.
* [kinêsis — movement, change, process](kinesis.md) - Motion or process, the exercise of a power; distinguished in Θ from *energeia*, the activity that is its own completion.
* [logos — account, formula, ratio](logos.md) - Speech, reason, account, definition, ratio — in metaphysical contexts usually the account that states an essence, or the proportion in which a kind of matter is mixed.
* [morphê — shape, form](morphe.md) - Form considered as the configuration of matter; used interchangeably with *eidos* in hylomorphic contexts.
* [on / onta — being, beings](on.md) - The participle of *einai*, "to be"; *ta onta* are "the things that are", the subject matter of first philosophy studied qua beings.
* [ousia — substance](ousia.md) - Literally "beingness", from the feminine participle of *einai*; the first category and the primary sense of being, translated *substantia* in Latin and "substance" ever after.
* [pollachôs legetai — said in many ways](pollachos-legetai.md) - Aristotle's formula for a term with several related senses rather than one definition — applied to being, to cause, to one, and to potentiality.
* [sophia — wisdom](sophia.md) - Knowledge of the primary causes and starting-points; one of Aristotle's four names for the inquiry the *Metaphysics* conducts.
* [telos — end, that for the sake of which](telos.md) - The goal or completion toward which a process runs; the final cause, and in Θ the reason actuality is prior in substance to potentiality.
* [to ti ên einai — essence](to-ti-en-einai.md) - "The what it was to be" for a thing; so awkward that the Roman translators coined *essentia* for the whole phrase, whence "essence".
* [tode ti — a this something](tode-ti.md) - A fully determinate individual, not further differentiable; the second condition on substance, and — crucially — not the same as being a particular.
