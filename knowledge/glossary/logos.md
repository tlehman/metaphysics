"A definition is an account (*logos*) that signifies an essence"
(*Topics* 102a3). Priority "in *logos*" is priority in definition, one of the
three senses in which
[actuality is prior to potentiality](../aristotle/priority-of-actuality.md).

Also the ratio: bronze is copper and tin in a certain *logos*, which is why a
kind of matter is itself formally analysable and so never a bare subject —
see [prime matter](../aristotle/prime-matter.md).
