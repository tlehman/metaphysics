The correlative of [hulê](hule.md). Η.6 pairs them explicitly: "there is on the
one hand matter and on the other shape (*morphê*), and the one is potentially
and the other actively" (1045a20–25).

See [hylomorphism](../aristotle/hylomorphism.md) and [eidos](eidos.md).
