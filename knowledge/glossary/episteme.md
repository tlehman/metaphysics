*Epistêmê* is of the [universal](katholou.md); particulars are indefinable, so
there is no science of Socrates, only of human beings. Set that against the
claim that reality's basic items are particular compounds and you have the
[primacy dilemma](../aristotle/primacy-dilemma.md).

M.10's resolution splits *epistêmê* itself into potential and actual — the
capacity is of what is universal, the activity of what is definite.
