Jonathan Bennett's edition. The title keeps Aristotle's phrase and changes its
sense: [first philosophy](../aristotle/first-philosophy.md) in Ε.1 is the study
of being qua being, while here it is what must be secured before any other
science can stand. Meditation I's dreaming argument is the wedge between the
order of knowing and the order of being that Aristotle's realism does not allow
— which is why it appears in the
[primacy dilemma](../aristotle/primacy-dilemma.md).
