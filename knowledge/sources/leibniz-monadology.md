Jonathan Bennett's edition of the 1714 text (*The Principles of Philosophy known
as Monadology*). §§31–32 state contradiction and sufficient reason as the two
principles of all reasoning — the source for
[the PSR](../ultimate-reality/principle-of-sufficient-reason.md) and the
Leibnizian branch of
[the cosmological argument](../ultimate-reality/cosmological-argument.md).
