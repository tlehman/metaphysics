Aristotle gives [form priority over matter](substantial-form.md), so we expect
[actuality](potentiality-and-actuality.md) to be prior to potentiality, and
Θ.8 (1049b4–5) supplies three senses of priority.

## 1. In account (logos)

We must cite the actuality to give the account of the corresponding
potentiality. "Visible" means *capable of being seen*; "buildable" means
*capable of being built* (1049b14–16). The definition of the capacity contains
its exercise, not conversely.

## 2. In time

Here potentiality looks prior — the wood precedes the table, the acorn precedes
the oak. Aristotle grants the particular case and denies the general one: "the
active that is the same in form, though not in number, is prior"
(1049b18–19). This acorn precedes this oak, but was itself preceded in time by
an actual oak that produced it, identical in species. The seed presupposes an
adult.

## 3. In substance — two arguments

**(a) From finality.** Things that come to be move toward an end. "The activity
is the end, and it is for the sake of this that the capacity is acquired. For
animals do not see in order that they may have sight, rather they have sight in
order that they may see … matter is potentially something because it may come
in the form of it" (1050a9–17). Form or actuality is the *telos* of natural
process, so actuality is a cause of a thing's realising its potential in more
than one of the [four senses](four-causes.md) at once.

**(b) From eternity — the "even stricter" argument** (1050b6–1051a2):

1. A potentiality is for either of a pair of opposites.
2. So whatever is capable of being is capable of not being.
3. What is capable of not being might possibly not be, and so is perishable.
4. Hence anything with mere potentiality to be is perishable.
5. What is eternal is imperishable, so nothing eternal can exist only
   potentially — the eternal must be fully actual.
6. The eternal is prior in substance to the perishable (it can exist without
   the perishable, not conversely; cf. Δ.11 1019a2).
7. Therefore what is actual is prior in substance to what is potential.

Step (5) is the hinge Λ picks up: an eternal cause of eternal motion cannot
have a *capacity* as its substance, or the motion would not be necessary. See
[the unmoved mover](unmoved-mover.md).
