Not a single composed work but a collection, and it reads like one: Books
Ζ.7–9 are almost certainly an insertion (Η.1's summary of Ζ skips straight
from Ζ.6 to Ζ.10), Book Κ overlaps Γ and Ε, and several
[aporiai](aristotelian-aporiai.md) raised in Β are never explicitly resolved.
Scholarship cites the books by Greek letter; the Roman numerals follow in
parentheses.

## Book map

| Book | Content | Concepts here |
|---|---|---|
| Α (I) | Wisdom; history of predecessors on causes | [first philosophy](first-philosophy.md), [four causes](four-causes.md) |
| α (II) | Short methodological preface | — |
| Β (III) | The catalogue of perplexities | [aporiai](aristotelian-aporiai.md) |
| Γ (IV) | Being qua being; the axioms | [being qua being](being-qua-being.md), [PNC](principle-of-non-contradiction.md), [pros hen](pros-hen-equivocity.md) |
| Δ (V) | Philosophical lexicon | feeds the [glossary](../glossary/index.md) |
| Ε (VI) | Division of the sciences; theology | [first philosophy](first-philosophy.md) |
| Ζ (VII) | The central books on substance | [substance](substance.md), [essence](essence.md), [substantial form](substantial-form.md) |
| Η (VIII) | Hylomorphic compounds; unity | [hylomorphism](hylomorphism.md), [unity of definition](unity-of-definition.md) |
| Θ (IX) | Potentiality and activity | [potentiality and actuality](potentiality-and-actuality.md) |
| Ι (X) | Unity, sameness, contrariety | [unity of definition](unity-of-definition.md) |
| Κ (XI) | Recapitulation of Γ, Ε and *Physics* | — |
| Λ (XII) | The prime mover | [unmoved mover](unmoved-mover.md) |
| Μ–Ν (XIII–XIV) | Against separate mathematical objects and Forms | [mathematical abstractionism](mathematical-abstractionism.md), [primacy dilemma](primacy-dilemma.md) |

The treatise presupposes two earlier works this bundle also tracks: the
[*Categories*](categories.md), whose ontology it revises, and the *Physics*,
whose matter/form apparatus it imports into the study of substance.
