The hub of the Aristotelian arc. Substances are unique in being *independent*:
qualities are qualities of substances, quantities the sizes substances come in,
relations the ways substances stand to one another. Everything in the other
nine [categories](categories.md) owes its existence to them.

Because "being" has a [pros hen](pros-hen-equivocity.md) unity centred on
substance, the ancient question "what is being?" *is* the question "what is
substance?" (Ζ.1, 1028b4).

## Which things, and what makes them so

Two questions, and Ζ separates them sharply. The *Categories* answered the
first with a negative criterion — primary substances are what is neither in nor
said of a subject — and gave examples: this man, this horse. Ζ.2 reopens the
list (bodies; surfaces, lines and points; Platonic Forms and mathematical
objects) and treats them all as live candidates.

But even knowing *that* something is a substance leaves open what *makes* it
one. To answer that is to identify **the substance of** the thing. Ζ.3 lists
four candidates:

| Candidate | Verdict | Where |
|---|---|---|
| [Subject](subject-criterion.md) | fails — leads to formless matter | Ζ.3 |
| [Essence](essence.md) | accepted, with qualification | Ζ.4–6 |
| [Universal](universals-and-substance.md) | attacked | Ζ.13 |
| Genus | attacked with the universal | Ζ.13 |

The answer Ζ converges on is that the substance of a thing is its
[form](substantial-form.md), which is its essence — and in Ζ.17 that form is
recast as the [cause of the thing's being](substantial-form.md) what it is.
Θ then reinterprets form as [actuality](potentiality-and-actuality.md), which
is what lets Λ reach a substance whose whole essence is activity: the
[unmoved mover](unmoved-mover.md).

The concept has a long afterlife: [Avicenna](../lineage/avicenna.md) and
[Aquinas](../lineage/aquinas.md) split it against
[existence](../ultimate-reality/essence-existence-distinction.md),
[Spinoza](../lineage/spinoza.md) reduces it to one, and contemporary
metaphysics reworks its priority claim as
[grounding](../being/grounding.md) and
[fundamentality](../being/fundamentality.md).
