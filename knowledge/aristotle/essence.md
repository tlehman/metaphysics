Aristotle's phrase *to ti ên einai* — literally "the what it was to be" for a
thing — so defeated his Roman translators that they coined *essentia* to render
the whole of it, and our word descends from that. He also uses the shorter
*to ti esti*, "the what it is."

Three notions lock together in the logical works and are reasserted in Ζ.4:

- **Definition** (*horismos*) — "an account that signifies an essence"
  (*Topics* 102a3).
- **Per se predication** ([kath' hauto](../glossary/kath-hauto.md)) — "what
  belongs to a thing in respect of itself belongs to it in its essence"
  (*Post. An.* 73a34–5).
- **Essence** — "the essence of each thing is what it is said to be
  intrinsically" (1029b14).

One defines *things, not words*. The definition of tiger does not give the
meaning of "tiger"; it says what it is to be a tiger.

## The qualification that does the work

Items in *every* category are definable, so items in every category have
essences — there is an essence of white and of musical as much as of man. But
because of [pros hen equivocity](pros-hen-equivocity.md) those are secondary:
"in the primary and unconditional way definition and the essence belong to
substances" (1030b4–6). Only primary essences are
[substance](substance.md).

Ζ.6 adds that whatever is "primary and spoken of in respect of itself" is *one
and the same as its essence*. An accidental unity — *pale man* — is not spoken
of in respect of itself, so it is not identical with its essence and does not
specify the what-it-is of any primary being. Essence in the primary sense
"will belong to things that are species of a genus and to nothing else"
(1030a11–12).

That last line tempts one to identify the substance of a thing with its
species. It should be resisted: the point is that a primary essence
*corresponds to* a species, and — decisively — *eidos* has changed meaning.
In the logical works it meant species (against genus); in the hylomorphic
context of Ζ it means [form](substantial-form.md) (against matter).

The essence/existence pair that medieval metaphysics builds on this is a
[later development](../ultimate-reality/essence-existence-distinction.md);
the modern quarrel over whether essence reduces to modality is
[here](../being/essence-and-modality.md).
