The terminus of the Aristotelian arc and the point where it becomes an account
of [ultimate reality](../ultimate-reality/ultimate-explanation.md).

## Why there must be one

Λ.6 runs on the identification of form with
[actuality](potentiality-and-actuality.md). Something with a capacity to move
things need not be exercising it, so positing eternal substances (as the
Platonists do with the [Forms](../ultimate-reality/theory-of-forms.md)) buys
nothing unless something in them can cause change — and even an *active*
substance is not enough "if the substance of it is a capacity. For then there
will not be *eternal* movement, since what is potentially may possibly not be.
There must, therefore, be such a starting-point, the very substance of which is
activity" (1071b12–22). Such substances must be without matter, since they must
be eternal.

Λ.8 asks how many such activities the astronomy requires and answers
forty-nine (1074a16). They form one coordinated system because there is a single
prime mover: things many in number have matter, "but the primary essence does
not have matter, since it is an actuality. The primary immovable mover,
therefore, is one both in account and in number" (1074a31–38).

## How it moves without being moved

"This is the way the object of desire and the intelligible object move things:
they move them without being moved" (1072a21–27). The primary heaven is moved
by the god as we are moved by a good we desire — final, not efficient,
causation. (That the heavens are therefore alive is argued in *De Caelo* II.12.)

## Thought thinking itself

Why is the god not in turn moved by whatever *it* contemplates? Λ.9: if its
substance were a capacity to understand rather than active understanding, then
what is understood would be more estimable than the understanding; and the mere
capacity belongs even to one who contemplates the worst thing. "It is itself,
therefore, that it understands, if indeed it is the most excellent thing, and
the active understanding is active understanding of active understanding"
(*hê noêsis noêseôs noêsis*, 1074b22–35).

Λ.7 supplies the content of that state: "the activity of understanding is
life, and he is that activity; and his intrinsic activity is life that is best
and eternal" (1072b18–28) — the state we are in when happiest, which the god is
always in. *Nicomachean Ethics* X.6–8 draws the ethical corollary, so practical
and theoretical wisdom share one first principle.

## Downstream

This is the ancestor of the [cosmological
argument](../ultimate-reality/cosmological-argument.md),
[pure actuality](../ultimate-reality/pure-actuality.md) and
[divine simplicity](../ultimate-reality/divine-simplicity.md) in
[Avicenna](../lineage/avicenna.md) and [Aquinas](../lineage/aquinas.md), and —
via the reading that being is unified "with reference to one thing" — of the
claim that first philosophy is theology
([first philosophy](first-philosophy.md), [primacy
dilemma](primacy-dilemma.md)).
