Also called *focal meaning*, after Owen. *Pros hen* is Greek for "in relation to
one."

The problem it solves is sharp. A single science requires a single kind with a
single definition. Compare "table": a dining table is a flat slab on legs, a
tide table is an arrangement of data in rows and columns, and there is no one
sense covering both. So there is no science of tables *qua* tables. If "being"
were ambiguous like that, [being qua being](being-qua-being.md) would be an
equally empty project.

Γ.2 denies the parallel. "Being" is *said in many ways*
([pollachôs legetai](../glossary/pollachos-legetai.md)) but the ways are
related. Aristotle's analogies:

- **Healthy** — a person *has* health, exercise is *productive of* health, a
  clear complexion is *symptomatic of* health. One central thing, health,
  possessed literally by only some of them.
- **Medical** — the same structure.

So with being. [Substances](substance.md) are beings in the primary sense; the
qualities and quantities that belong to substances are beings only because they
are appropriately related to those. Any account of the being of anything must
therefore refer to substance — which is precisely how a universal science of
being turns out to have a determinate subject after all.

Λ pushes the same structure one level further: beings are unified not "in
accord with one thing," as a single genus would be, but "with reference to one
thing," a divine substance that is essentially activity
([unmoved mover](unmoved-mover.md)).
