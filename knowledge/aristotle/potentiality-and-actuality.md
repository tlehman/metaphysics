[Hylomorphism](hylomorphism.md) is introduced synchronically — this stuff, so
arranged, now. Θ applies the same distinction *across time*, and in doing so
converts it into the pair [dunamis](../glossary/dunamis.md) /
[energeia](../glossary/energeia.md).

## Two senses of dunamis

- **Power.** Strictly, a *dunamis* is the power to produce a change: having
  "a starting-point of change in another thing or in itself insofar as it is
  other" (Θ.1, 1046a12). Its exercise is a
  [kinêsis](../glossary/kinesis.md), a process — the housebuilder's craft
  exercised as housebuilding.
- **Potentiality.** The sense Aristotle mainly wants, related not to movement
  but to activity (Θ.6, 1048a25): not a power to change something else but a
  thing's *capacity to be in a different and more completed state*.

Potentiality in this second sense is **indefinable** (1048a37); the idea is to
be grasped from cases. Activity is to potentiality as "what is awake is in
relation to what is asleep, and what is seeing is in relation to what has its
eyes closed but has sight, and what has been shaped out of the matter is in
relation to the matter" (1048b1–3).

## The wood and the table

A piece of wood is potentially a table and potentially a bowl. Carve it, and it
is tempting to say the wood is now actually a table. Aristotle may mean
something subtler: even the wood *composing the finished table* is still only
potentially a table, because it is not the wood *qua* wood that is a table but
the wood *qua* table. Considered as matter, a thing remains potentially what it
is the matter of. (A contemporary reader would say the wood *constitutes* the
table without being identical to it.)

That asymmetry is exactly what makes the [unity of
definition](unity-of-definition.md) tractable, and what
[grounds actuality's priority](priority-of-actuality.md) — which is in turn
what Λ needs to reach a substance whose essence just *is* activity, the
[unmoved mover](unmoved-mover.md).
