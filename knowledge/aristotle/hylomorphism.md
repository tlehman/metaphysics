From [hulê](../glossary/hule.md) (matter) and [morphê](../glossary/morphe.md)
(shape, form). Synchronically: the matter of a substance is the stuff it is
composed of, the form is the way that stuff is put together so the whole can
perform its characteristic function ([ergon](../glossary/ergon.md)).

## Generation

Ζ.7–9 asks how matter and form figure in coming-to-be, and the answer is the
same for natural objects and artefacts. We do not produce the matter — that
regresses. We do not produce the form — out of what? We *put the form into the
matter* and produce the compound (1033a30–b9). Both matter and form pre-exist
(1034b12); what changes is that they are joined.

The moving cause is the form. In craft, "the form is in the soul" of the
craftsman: the builder holds the design and enmatters it (1032b23, 1034a24). In
natural generation the form is in the parent — "human begets human"
(1033b29–31), begetter and begotten one in form though not in number.

## What is produced is named for its form

A house, a man — not bricks, not flesh. If gold is the matter of a statue,
gold was there at the start and so did not come to be; the statue did. Hence we
should call it not "gold" but "golden" (1033a7), and refuse to identify the
statue with the gold. The same refusal, run diachronically, is what
[potentiality and actuality](potentiality-and-actuality.md) formalises.

The essence of a compound is accordingly its form, not its matter: "by form I
mean the essence of each thing and the primary substance" (1032b1). That is
the route to [substantial form](substantial-form.md) as the answer to
[what substance is](substance.md).
