The bridge from the Aristotelian arc into
[the philosophy of mathematics](../mathematics/philosophy-of-mathematics.md).

Books M and N are not really a philosophy of mathematics; they are a campaign
against two opponents — Platonists who posit immutable eternal substances
beyond sensible ones, and Pythagoreans who identify numbers with sensible
substances. Aristotle's own view has to be assembled from them plus
*Posterior Analytics*, *De Anima* iii.6–8, *Metaphysics* Ζ.10–11, Μ.1–3 and
*Physics* ii.2.

## The machinery

- **Removal** (*aphairesis*, often "abstraction"). Start from perceptible
  magnitudes — bodies, surfaces, edges, times, sounds — and take away the
  attributes that are not part of the science. In the *Analytics* what is
  removed from a particular figure is its particularity, leaving a universal;
  elsewhere what is left may be a particular quasi-fictional entity, and its
  status is where the controversy lives.
- **As separated** (*hôs kekhôrismenon*). Only substances are genuinely
  [separate](../glossary/choriston.md). But we can speak of a triangle — merely
  the limit of a body — *as if* it were separate. *Physics* II.2: "they are
  separable in the understanding from movement, and so their being separated
  makes no difference, nor does any falsehood result from it" (193b31–35).
  This is fictionalism about separation, not about mathematics.
- **Qua** ([hêi](../glossary/hei.md)). "*X* qua *Y*" is elliptical for "*X* in
  the respect that *X* is *Y*." To study a bronze triangle *qua* triangle is to
  study what follows from being a triangle; bronze never enters. The same
  operator that defines [being qua being](being-qua-being.md) defines what the
  mathematician does.
- **Intelligible matter** (*hulê noêtê*). What pure geometrical objects, not
  realised in bronze or stone, are made of — also the tool Η.6 borrows for
  [the unity of definition](unity-of-definition.md).

## The metaphysical payoff

Mathematical objects come out as *dependent* entities — abstractions from
perceptible sublunary beings, not substantial starting-points. That is the last
step in Λ's unification of being: attributes depend on substances, compounds
depend on forms, numbers depend on compounds
([primacy dilemma](primacy-dilemma.md)).

Modern readers have found here an ancestor of
[nominalism](../mathematics/mathematical-nominalism.md),
[fictionalism](../mathematics/fictionalism.md), and physicalist accounts of
[abstract objects](../mathematics/abstract-objects.md) — the standing
alternative to [mathematical platonism](../mathematics/mathematical-platonism.md).
Ancient and medieval commentators pulled the other way, reading the objects as
mental, which made Aristotle safe for
[Neoplatonism](../ultimate-reality/neoplatonic-emanation.md) at the cost of
making him a psychologist.
