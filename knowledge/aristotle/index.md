# aristotle

The seed. Aristotle's *Metaphysics* worked through section by section — the categories, the search for substance across Books Ζ–Θ, and the theology of Λ — with every Bekker citation traced to the SEP entry this bundle was built from.

## Concepts

* [The Aporiai of Book Β](aristotelian-aporiai.md) - Aristotle's catalogue of the perplexities that "tie our thinking up in knots" — the agenda the *Metaphysics* sets itself, argued on both sides in Β and taken up, though not always visibly resolved, in the later books.
* [Being Qua Being](being-qua-being.md) - Aristotle's characterization of first philosophy — not a study of some special subject matter called "being qua being," but the study of ordinary beings under the most general aspect available, in so far as they are.
* [The Ten Categories](categories.md) - The exhaustive division of beings in Aristotle's *Categories* into substance plus nine dependent kinds — quality, quantity, relation and the rest — with substance given ontological privilege because everything else exists only "in" a subject.
* [Definition and the Method of Division](definition-by-division.md) - Definition by genus and differentia, refined by Ζ.12's rule that each division must take the differentia *of the differentia* — which collapses a chain of differentiae into one but does not, by itself, make a definition one thing.
* [Essence (to ti ên einai)](essence.md) - The what-it-was-to-be of a thing — what a thing is said to be intrinsically, expressed by its definition; Aristotle's preliminary answer to \"what is substance?\", qualified so that only the essences of species-level kinds count as primary.
* [First Philosophy](first-philosophy.md) - Aristotle's name for the inquiry into the primary causes and principles of things — not the branch studied first, but the one at the highest level of generality, which he also calls wisdom, the study of being qua being, and theology.
* [The Four Causes](four-causes.md) - The four senses of aitia — material, formal, efficient, final — which are four kinds of answer to "why?", and which for a living thing collapse into one, since its soul is cause in three of the four ways.
* [Hylomorphism](hylomorphism.md) - The analysis of an individual substance as a compound of matter (hulê) and form (morphê/eidos) — imported from the *Physics* into the study of substance, where it gives *this is a horse* the predicative structure the *Categories* denied it.
* [Aristotle on Mathematical Objects](mathematical-abstractionism.md) - Mathematical objects are not separate substances but perceptible magnitudes considered "as separated" — reached by removal (aphairesis) and studied qua quantity — which makes numbers dependent on compounds and is the ancient ancestor of nominalism and fictionalism.
* [Aristotle's *Metaphysics*](metaphysics-treatise.md) - The fourteen books, cited by Greek letter, that the first-century editor assembled from smaller selections — the treatise this bundle is seeded from, and the source of nearly every concept in the Aristotelian arc.
* [Potentiality and Actuality](potentiality-and-actuality.md) - Book Θ's diachronic recasting of matter and form: matter is dunamis, the capacity to be in a more completed state, and form is energeia/entelecheia, the activity that state consists in — a distinction Aristotle says is graspable only from cases, not definable.
* [The Primacy Dilemma](primacy-dilemma.md) - If the starting-points of science are universal they are not substances, and if they are particular they are not knowable — a realist's crisis, since science's first principles must be reality's building blocks, resolved only in M.10 by the god who is both at once.
* [Prime Matter](prime-matter.md) - The wholly indeterminate substrate left when every predicate has been stripped from a hylomorphic compound in thought — the terminus of the subject criterion, which Aristotle produces as a reductio and never clearly commits to.
* [The Principle of Non-Contradiction](principle-of-non-contradiction.md) - The axiom that the same thing cannot at the same time belong and not belong to the same thing in the same respect — which Aristotle argues cannot be proved, since every proof presupposes it, but can be shown to be undeniable.
* [The Priority of Actuality](priority-of-actuality.md) - Θ.8's argument that actuality is prior to potentiality in account, in time and in substance — the last by way of finality and by way of eternity, since what is merely potential is capable of not being, hence perishable.
* [Pros Hen Equivocity](pros-hen-equivocity.md) - Aristotle's solution to the threat that "being" is merely ambiguous — its senses are neither univocal nor sheerly homonymous but all refer to one central case, which is what makes a single science of being possible.
* [The Subject Criterion and Its Failure](subject-criterion.md) - Ζ.3's test — the substance of x is the ultimate subject x is predicated of — which drives all the way down to formless matter and is rejected, because substance must be both separable and a "this something" and matter is never both at once.
* [Substance (ousia)](substance.md) - The category of independent beings, and the central case to which every other sense of "being" refers — so that Aristotle's question "what is being?" becomes "what is substance?", the question Books Ζ, Η and Θ spend themselves on.
* [Substantial Form](substantial-form.md) - The form predicated of a substance's matter, identified with its essence and with the cause of its being the kind of thing it is — Ζ's answer to "what is the substance of x?", and the pivot on which the whole treatise turns.
* [The Unity of Definition](unity-of-definition.md) - Why is a definiendum one thing when its definiens is a combination of terms? Ζ.12's method of division does not answer it; Η.6 does, by treating genus as matter and differentia as form, so the two are one as potentiality and actuality are one.
* [Are Substantial Forms Universals?](universals-and-substance.md) - Ζ.13 argues that no universal is a substance, but Ζ has already argued that substance is form and that forms are universals — the largest and most disputed interpretive crux in the *Metaphysics*.
* [The Unmoved Mover](unmoved-mover.md) - Book Λ's eternal substance whose very essence is activity — moving the heavens as an object of desire moves a lover, and consisting in thought thinking itself, since anything else it contemplated would be more estimable than it.
